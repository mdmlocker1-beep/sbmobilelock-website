Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

📅 Effective Date: September 16, 2026
At SB Mobile Lock, we strive to ensure complete satisfaction with our services. This Refund & Cancellation Policy outlines the terms under which refunds are granted for our software licenses and subscription plans.

1. General Refund Policy

SB Mobile Lock provides software-based services in the form of device licenses and subscription plans. Due to the nature of digital software services, all sales are generally considered final. However, we offer refunds under specific eligible circumstances described below.

All refund requests must be submitted within the applicable timeframe and will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis by our support team.

2. Refund Eligibility

2.1 Eligible for Full Refund

You are eligible for a full refund if:

  • You request a refund within 7 days of your initial subscription purchase and have not activated more than 2 device licenses
  • A technical error on our part resulted in a duplicate charge
  • You were charged for a service that was not delivered or provisioned
  • Our platform was non-functional for more than 72 consecutive hours due to issues on our end during your paid subscription period

2.2 Eligible for Partial Refund

You may be eligible for a pro-rata partial refund if:

  • You cancel an annual or long-term subscription with more than 3 months remaining and fewer than 30% of your total licenses used
  • We make a material change to the Platform that significantly reduces its functionality and you object within 14 days of notification

2.3 Not Eligible for Refund

Refunds will not be issued for:

  • Requests made after the 7-day refund window for new subscriptions
  • Licenses where more than 2 devices have been activated
  • Subscription periods already consumed
  • Accounts suspended due to violation of our Terms of Service
  • Free trial conversions or promotional/discounted licenses
  • Dissatisfaction with features that were accurately described before purchase
  • Inability to use the service due to incompatible device hardware or software on the customer's device

4. Cancellation Policy

4.1 Monthly Subscriptions

You may cancel a monthly subscription at any time. Your access will continue until the end of the current billing cycle. No refund will be issued for the remaining days of the current month.

4.2 Annual / Long-Term Subscriptions

Annual subscriptions may be cancelled at any time. If cancelled within 7 days of purchase and fewer than 2 licenses are activated, a full refund is available. For cancellations after the 7-day window, a pro-rata refund may apply per Section 2.2.

4.3 License Packs

License packs (one-time purchases) are generally non-refundable once the license codes have been delivered. If a license code was not functional due to a technical error on our part, we will provide a replacement or refund at our discretion.

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please follow these steps:

  • Contact us via email at sbmobilelock@gmail.com or WhatsApp at +91 85138 72639 with the subject line: "Refund Request"
  • Provide your registered mobile number or email, order/invoice number, and reason for the refund request
  • Our support team will review your request within 3–5 business days and respond with a decision
  • If approved, refunds will be processed to the original payment method within 7–10 business days

6. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be credited to the original payment method used during purchase:

  • UPI / Bank Transfer: 2–5 business days
  • Credit / Debit Card: 5–10 business days (depending on your bank)
  • Net Banking: 3–7 business days

SB Mobile Lock is not responsible for delays caused by your bank or payment processor.

7. Disputes

If you are dissatisfied with our refund decision, you may escalate the matter by emailing sbmobilelock@gmail.com with the subject "Refund Dispute". We will make every reasonable effort to resolve disputes amicably within 14 business days.

8. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Refund Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of our services after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact Us

For refund requests, questions, or concerns, please reach out to us directly.
