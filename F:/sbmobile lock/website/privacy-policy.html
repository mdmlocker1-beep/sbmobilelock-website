Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

📅 Effective Date: September 16, 2026
SB Mobile Lock ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application, website, and services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you register for our services, contact us, or fill out forms, we may collect:

  • Full name, business name, and contact details
  • Email address and mobile phone number
  • Business address and location details
  • Bank account or payment information (processed securely)
  • Device IMEI numbers and device-related data entered by retailers

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use our App or website, we may automatically collect:

  • Device information (model, OS version, unique device identifiers)
  • GPS/location data (with your permission) for device tracking features
  • Usage data and app interaction logs
  • IP address and browser type (website)
  • Crash reports and diagnostic data

1.3 Customer Device Data (Retailer-Managed)

Retailers using SB Mobile Lock install our software on customer devices. Data collected from those devices (location, lock status, etc.) is managed by the retailer and processed by us on their behalf. Customers must be informed by the retailer of this data collection at the point of sale.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the SB Mobile Lock platform
  • Enable device locking, tracking, and EMI management features
  • Send EMI payment reminders and notifications
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions
  • Provide customer support and respond to enquiries
  • Improve our app, website, and services
  • Comply with legal obligations under Indian law
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, and unauthorized access

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only with:

  • Authorized Distributors: To manage retailer accounts within the distribution network
  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors (e.g., cloud hosting, SMS/email services) who process data on our behalf under confidentiality agreements
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law, court order, or government regulation in India
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, where users will be notified

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit
  • Encrypted storage of sensitive data
  • Role-based access controls
  • Regular security audits

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information but cannot guarantee its absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us. Certain data may be retained for legal, regulatory, or dispute resolution purposes.

6. Your Rights

Under applicable Indian data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing (where applicable)
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact us at sbmobilelock@gmail.com.

7. Location Data

Our app uses GPS location data to enable device tracking features for retailers. This data is collected only when the feature is active and is accessible only to the authorized retailer who sold the device. End-customers are informed of location tracking at the time of device setup.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website or app may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of India. Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in West Bengal, India.
